## What it does

- **Pomodoro timer** with the 25/5/20 cycle. Earn 1 XP per focus minute and level up through 10 ranks.
- **Intensity meter** that rises while you work and drops on breaks. At 100 % the app enforces a rest.
- **Audio mixer** with lo-fi tracks and ambient sounds (rain, café, fireplace, forest, ocean, train).
- **Stats and insights**: weekly charts, a 365-day heatmap, streaks, your most productive hours and an AI focus coach.
- **Challenges, achievements and themes**, including a custom theme creator.
- **Widgets, Live Activities and cloud sync** across devices.

## How it's built

- SwiftUI with MVVM, iOS 17+, async/await
- Supabase for auth (Email OTP, Apple, Google, Discord) and cloud sync
- StoreKit 2 subscriptions for Pro
- WidgetKit and ActivityKit for widgets and Live Activities
- Local-first: works offline and syncs when connected
- Landing page on Vercel at [focusxp.site](https://focusxp.site)
